# Two-Minute Video Script

Target length: exactly 2 minutes.

## 0:00-0:15 Problem

Researchers do not only need to read papers. They need to understand what an entire research field knows. That means comparing methods, datasets, metrics, findings, limitations, contradictions, and research gaps across many studies.

## 0:15-0:30 Existing Solutions

Current research tools are useful for search, summaries, citation maps, and evidence lookup. But most tools still optimize individual research tasks. They do not continuously build a structured model of scientific understanding across papers.

## 0:30-0:50 Burhan

Burhan is an Agentic AI Research Scientist. It reads research papers, extracts structured scientific knowledge, and builds a living Research Digital Twin. The twin becomes Burhan's intelligence layer: a model of papers, methods, datasets, findings, limitations, evidence, and gaps.

## 0:50-1:15 Demo

In the demo, we start with papers from one research domain. Burhan parses the papers, extracts entities such as methods, datasets, metrics, findings, and limitations, then connects them inside a Scientific Knowledge Graph. When a new paper is added, the twin can update with new evidence and relationships.

## 1:15-1:35 Architecture

Burhan uses a multi-agent architecture: a Research Planning Agent, Scientific Understanding Agent, Knowledge Extraction Agent, Research Twin Builder, Evidence Verification Agent, Cross-Paper Reasoning Agent, Research Gap Discovery Agent, and Research Assistant. RAG supports evidence retrieval, while the Research Digital Twin drives the intelligence.

## 1:50-2:00 Closing

Burhan turns scattered papers into evidence-backed scientific intelligence. Our next step is to harden extraction, expand the graph, and prepare a stronger Research Digital Twin for the FARQ Grand Final.
