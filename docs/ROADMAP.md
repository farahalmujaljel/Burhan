# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Prototype

Goal: demonstrate that Burhan can convert research papers into structured scientific knowledge.

Deliverables:

- focused paper set;
- PDF parsing or pre-parsed demo text;
- extraction schema;
- sample extracted entities;
- initial Scientific Knowledge Graph;
- basic dashboard or visualization.

## Phase 2: FARQ MVP

Goal: prove the full paper-to-twin workflow.

Deliverables:

- Research Planning Agent;
- Scientific Understanding Agent;
- Knowledge Extraction Agent;
- Research Twin Builder;
- Evidence Verification Agent;
- cross-paper comparison;
- research gap cards;
- grounded assistant response;
- 2-minute FARQ video.

## Phase 3: Research Intelligence

Goal: improve reasoning quality and make the twin more useful for researchers.

Deliverables:

- contradiction detection;
- method and dataset comparison views;
- trend detection;
- stronger evidence verification;
- confidence and review indicators;
- improved graph traversal.

## Phase 4: Platform Hardening

Goal: move from hackathon prototype to a reliable research platform.

Deliverables:

- larger paper collections;
- Qdrant-backed evidence retrieval;
- Neo4j graph persistence;
- LangGraph workflow observability;
- better PDF parsing through Docling;
- user feedback and correction loops.

## Phase 5: Continuous Research Digital Twin

Goal: continuously evolve the Research Digital Twin as new papers are added.

Deliverables:

- incremental paper ingestion;
- duplicate entity resolution;
- graph update history;
- research gap evolution;
- integration with scholarly data sources;
- exportable research intelligence reports.
