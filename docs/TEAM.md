## Team Positioning

The team is organized around the architecture of Burhan:

- Product leadership defines what the Agentic AI Research Scientist should do.
- AI engineering converts papers into structured knowledge.
- Knowledge graph engineering turns extracted knowledge into a Scientific Knowledge Graph.
- Backend integration connects parsing, agents, graph storage, and retrieval.
- Frontend and visualization make the Research Digital Twin understandable to researchers and judges.
